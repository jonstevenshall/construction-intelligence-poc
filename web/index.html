An experiment in helping anyone turn a rough construction brief into a clearer scope and an explained, indicative cost range.

The problem

You know what you want to achieve, but not every technical detail, hidden cost or local restriction. Getting a useful early budget can mean knowing which questions to ask first.

The approach

Describe the work in plain English. The agent clarifies what matters, searches for relevant local context and restrictions, and explores their cost implications. It explains assumptions, sources and what is still unpriced as the estimate develops.

Add new datasets for intelligent ingestion

Describe what the fields mean, including units and relationships. Link new records into the evidence graph so the agent can discover, use and explain additional sources.

View the source

Earthworks is the first example. The jobs, suppliers, bids and prices are synthetic: this demonstrates the concept, rather than real market pricing or contractor offers.
